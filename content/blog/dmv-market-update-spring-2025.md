## Spring Market Overview

The DMV real estate market enters spring 2025 with cautious optimism. After two years of compressed inventory and double-digit appreciation, we're finally seeing more homes come to market — giving buyers a bit more breathing room without sacrificing the demand that has made this region so resilient.

### Washington DC

Median prices in DC have stabilized around **$699,000**, up 3.2% year-over-year. The most competitive neighborhoods remain Capitol Hill, Logan Circle, and Navy Yard, where well-priced homes still receive multiple offers within the first weekend. Condos in the $400K–$550K range are moving fastest, driven by remote workers returning to city living and first-time buyers priced out of the suburbs.

### Maryland

Montgomery County continues to lead suburban Maryland with a median price of **$620,000** and an average of just 18 days on market. Bethesda and Chevy Chase remain perennial favorites, but savvy buyers are looking east — Silver Spring and Takoma Park offer significantly more value with excellent Metro access.

Prince George's County is the story of 2025. Median prices have climbed to **$420,000**, up 6.8% YoY, as buyers recognize the value and proximity to DC that the county offers.

### Northern Virginia

NoVA remains the tightest submarket in the region. Arlington and Alexandria see homes go under contract in under 10 days on average. The expansion of tech industry employment in the corridor has kept demand elevated even as mortgage rates hover above 6.5%.

## What This Means for You

**If you're buying:** Get pre-approved *before* you start touring. In competitive pockets, you need to be ready to submit within 24 hours. Work with your agent to understand the specific inventory dynamics in your target neighborhoods.

**If you're selling:** Well-priced, move-in ready homes are still achieving strong prices. But overpriced listings are sitting — buyers have become more selective as affordability remains stretched. Proper preparation and pricing strategy matter more than ever.
